Famous Indian Foods

Indian cuisine is famous for its spices, flavours and variety. Every region has its own special dishes. Indian food includes delicious meals, street food and traditional desserts.

Five Famous Indian Dishes

  1. Butter Chicken
  2. Masala Dosa
  3. Biryani
  4. Chole Bhature
  5. Gulab Jamun

Descriptions of Famous Dishes

Biryani

Famous Places in India

1. Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal in Agra is a beautiful white marble monument.

India Gate is a famous war memorial located in New Delhi.

3. Goa Beaches

Goa is famous for its beautiful beaches and scenic coastline.

4. Jaipur City Palace

Jaipur is known for its royal palaces and rich Rajasthani culture.

5. Kerala Backwaters

Kerala is famous for its peaceful backwaters and greenery.
